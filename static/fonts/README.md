Self-hosted web fonts: the Latin and Latin Extended subsets of Inter,
Fraunces and JetBrains Mono, as served by Google Fonts. All three are licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).

src/utils/fontFaces.js holds the @font-face rules; gatsby-ssr.js inlines
them into every page's <head>.
